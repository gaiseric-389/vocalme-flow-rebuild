# Growth Audit — VocalMe

**Live:** https://gaiseric-389.github.io/vocalme-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas for VocalMe: AI Music Generator, plus a clickable prototype of the onboarding and paywall rebuilt in VocalMe's own design. Only the sequence changes; the art direction is VocalMe's.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
|:-:|:--|:--|
| 01 | Offer your 7-day pass when they close the paywall | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Name what they are making on the paywall | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Ask for notifications when a track is cooking | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Swap the review request for your 4.6 stars | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Let them hear it before the price | CR +10–15% |

Combined potential: CR to purchase +25%, revenue up to +30%.

**Grounding.** Every price, rating, feature line and image comes from VocalMe's own App Store listing and in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for VocalMe.
